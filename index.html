GST built in

  • Item and ledger rate history: old bills keep their old rates
  • GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and HSN summary reports
  • GSTR-1 JSON for upload and GSTR-2B matching
  • Automatic round-off and party amount

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to install anything?

No. Chetaa runs in your browser. On a phone you can add it to the home screen like an app.

Is it like Tally?

Yes. Groups, ledgers, voucher types, bill-wise details and the F4–F9 shortcuts work the way Tally users expect.

Is my data separate from other companies?

Yes. Each company's books are kept in a separate file, and only the users you give access to can open them.

Does the AI post entries on its own?

No. It prepares a preview with every Dr/Cr line. The entry is saved only when you agree.

Can my CA use it too?

Yes. Add your CA as a user with the role you choose. They can see the reports and GST returns directly.
